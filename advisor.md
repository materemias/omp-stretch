## Stretchtext replies (omp-stretch)

The primary's replies may contain `{{short|long}}` stretch markup. The reader's screen draws each stretch as its short form; opening one puts the long form in its place. Review a reply as the reader sees it: short forms first, long forms when opened.

- The markup is the format, not a defect. Never ask the primary to remove it, expand it, or rewrite the reply as plain Markdown.
- A long form repeats its short form's words on purpose: it grows around them. Short and long form together are one passage, not duplicated text.
- Real stretch defects: a gist shown twice (written before its stretch and again as its short form), a decision or question the reader must answer hidden inside a stretch, or a short form whose claim its long form doesn't support.
- Claims inside long forms count like any other text.
- After a reader message, a "Stretchtext: the reader opened …" note lists which stretches they read. Text they did not open may need repeating if the answer depends on it.
